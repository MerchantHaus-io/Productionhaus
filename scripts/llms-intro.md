# MerchantHaus

> MerchantHaus (merchanthaus.io) gives U.S. merchants, ISOs, mid-market and enterprise organizations a unified payment gateway to accept credit and debit cards, ACH/eCheck, digital wallets, contactless and secure pay links online, in-store and on mobile. It is powered by NMI, priced month to month, and backed by U.S.-based human support.

Key facts:

- Company: MerchantHaus LLC, 1209 Mountain Road Pl NE Ste N, Albuquerque, NM 87110, United States. Phone +1-505-600-6042, email support@merchanthaus.io.
- Payment processing services are provided by NMI Payments, a registered ISO/MSP of Merrick Bank, Woodbury, NY. MerchantHaus LLC provides payment technology and support services.
- Payment methods: all major credit and debit cards, ACH/eCheck, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay, contactless, QR codes and 135+ currencies.
- Channels: card-present terminals and SmartPOS devices, Tap-to-Mobile (phone as a terminal), online checkout, hosted payment pages, invoices and pay links, recurring billing.
- Security: PCI DSS compliant, network tokenization, customer vault, end-to-end encryption, Kount AI fraud scoring and 3-D Secure.
- Integrations: 200+ commerce platforms and systems, including Shopify, QuickBooks, Salesforce, HubSpot, Clover, Magento, Wix, Squarespace, Zoho CRM, Keap and GoHighLevel. Merchants can keep their existing processor and plug the MerchantHaus gateway into it.
- Pricing: month-to-month with no long-term contract or early termination fee. Gateway plans are Foundation ($59/month), Growth ($99/month, adds Kount AI fraud management), Scale ($149/month, adds Level III Advantage for B2B) and custom Enterprise pricing; annual billing saves about 17%.
- Onboarding: proposal within 1 business day of receiving a processing statement, approval typically in 1–3 business days (about 2 weeks for higher-risk businesses), live within 1 business day of approval; about a week start to finish. Developers get a free sandbox on day one.
